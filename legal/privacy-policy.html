Genesis AI Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 31, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes the policies and procedures of XPDevs (James Turner) ("we," "our," or "us") regarding the handling of your information when you use the Genesis AI application ("the Service").

Crucially, Genesis AI is a client-side application. We do not collect, store, or transmit your chat conversations to any external servers.

1. The Core Principle: Local Data Storage

1.1. No Server Collection of Chat Data

Genesis AI is a client-side application. We strictly confirm that we do not collect, store, or transmit your chat messages, prompts, or the AI's responses to any server operated by XPDevs. All processing related to the AI model and your conversations happens directly in your web browser.

1.2. Local Storage

Your chat history, application settings, and other session-specific data are stored locally on your device using your browser’s storage mechanisms (such as LocalStorage or IndexedDB). This data is not accessible to us or to any third party through the Service. The persistence and security of this data are dependent on your browser settings and the security of your device.

1.3. Automated Content Filtering (Local Processing)

For safety and to enforce our Terms of Use, all text input is checked against an internal list of prohibited words or phrases (e.g., hate speech, threats, illegal content). This scanning process is done entirely on your device before the message is sent to the AI model. No information about this filtering process or the content of the blocked message is transmitted externally.

2. Information We May Have Visibility To

While we do not collect chat data, some non-chat-related information may be processed by necessary third-party services:

2.1. Hosting Provider Data

The Service is hosted on GitHub Pages. As a third-party service, GitHub may automatically collect standard web log information (such as your IP address, browser type, access times, and pages viewed) when you access the Service. This collection is governed by GitHub's privacy policy.

2.2. Cookies and Other Technologies

We may use necessary browser storage (e.g., LocalStorage) to save basic application preferences, such as your theme choice or current view. We do not use tracking cookies for targeted advertising or detailed profiling.

3. Your Data Control and Deletion Rights

3.1. Data Deletion

You can permanently delete all locally stored data, including all chat history, at any time by using the dedicated "Delete All Chats" function available within the application settings. Deleting the application's local storage or clearing your browser cache will also permanently delete this data.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the updated Policy on this page and revising the "Effective Date" at the top. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
